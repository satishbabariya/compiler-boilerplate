      326 -

          -n come to a

          -lution to the use

      351 -        that>." (Assuming the reader will think the example is
          - representative of                                            
      352 -        an important class of use cases.)                     
      353 -    -   Very helpful: "If we go with approach &lt;X>, it helps
          - with problem                                                 
      354 -        &lt;P> in &lt;this example> of use case &lt;A>. It doe
          -sn't help with use                                            
      355 -        case &lt;B>, but that can be better solved by &lt;Y>,

      356 -        &lt;other example>."

      358 -        problem / I don't see how it applies in &lt;this other

      366 -meta level as that can seem like you're evading the question.
